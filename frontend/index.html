Breakout Screener Guide

How to use the 2 tabs, strategy rules & trade execution

📈 What is this App?

This is a professional technical analysis screener designed for Indian stocks (NSE Nifty 500). It solves two main trading problems:

  • Validate Strategy Rules: Test your breakout filters against 1 year of real historical data before risking capital.
  • Pre-Market & Real-Time Trade Setup: Find stocks setting up for a breakout and calculate exact Entry, Stop-Loss, and Position Sizing.

🧪 Tab 1: Backtest Tab

Simulates your strategy over the past 12 months (252+ trading days) across 500 stocks:

  • Forward Returns: Tracks performance at 5D, 10D, 20D, and 30D intervals.
  • Score Bucket Analysis: Proves whether higher Strength Scores (80-100) yield higher win rates.
  • Monthly Distribution: Shows when breakouts happen most frequently.

⚡ Tab 2: Live Scan Tab

Scans current market prices and creates actionable trade plans for today:

  • Trade Execution Cards: Shows exact Entry Trigger (₹), Stop-Loss (₹), Target 1 (1:2 R:R), Target 2 (1:3 R:R), and Share Quantity.
  • "Why Selected?" Modal: Click any card to inspect the exact math, volume surge, 200 DMA status, BB Squeeze, and RSI.
  • Google Finance Direct Links: Click SYMBOL ↗ to open stock chart in Google Finance.

⚡ Confirmed Breakout vs. Near Breakout Radar

Choose your Scan Target in the top configuration panel based on your trading style:

Confirmed Breakout
Close > Resistance

Flagged after the stock has already broken out and closed above resistance today.

Action: Market Buy or buy pullbacks. Best for EOD momentum traders.
Near Breakout Radar
Close <= Resistance (0.5%–3% below)

Flagged before the breakout occurs while consolidating right under resistance.

Action: Set GTT Buy Order at Resistance + 0.1% so your broker buys automatically when price crosses.

📋 How to Place GTT Trigger Orders

  1. Run Live Scan in Near Breakout Radar mode.
  2. Click 📋 Copy GTT on your chosen stock card.
  3. Open your broker (Zerodha Kite, Dhan, Groww, or AngelOne).
  4. Go to GTT / Create Trigger Order for the stock.
  5. Paste or enter the Trigger Price, Stop Loss, and Quantity provided by the card.
  6. Submit order. When price breaks out during market hours, your order executes hands-free!
